## Key scenes (landscape 16:9)

### `palace-frozen.png`: the coronation courtyard after the curse
```
Wide establishing shot of a vast granite temple courtyard at noon, three thousand citizens turned to fine-grained pink-grey granite mid-celebration, the folds of their cotton clothing carved in stone, a woman's handful of thrown rice frozen in the air in a fan, stone temple elephants with bright untouched gold bells hanging tilted mid-swing, saffron silk canopies still rolled on their poles, strings of white jasmine and orange crossandra flowers between pillars, a prince on a raised pavilion holding a gold crown above his own head, the sun fixed at its highest point casting hard short shadows tinted blue-violet, dust motes hanging motionless in the air, the 13-tier black granite Mirror Temple tower in the background, 24mm lens, eye level
```

### `mirror-temple.png`: the Mirror Temple from below
```
Low-angle view up the north face of a 66-metre 13-tier pyramidal Dravidian vimana in dark charnockite granite, modelled on Brihadeeswarar Thanjavur, each tier ringed with miniature domed shrines and barrel-vaulted pavilions, a moulded plinth with Tamil inscription bands at the base, at the summit a broken gold finial and a slow halo of violet light, dozens of black glass shards hanging motionless in the air around the top, bleached noon sky shading to bruised violet at the zenith, a tiny human figure climbing a cornice halfway up for scale, 24mm lens, slight dust haze
```

### `mayans-door.png`
```
Close shot of a four-metre circular stone door set flush into black granite at the top of a temple tower, carved in three concentric rotating rings of walking elephants, dancers and stars, small mason's marks cut into each ring, a worn Grantha inscription above, a young man's dusty hand pressed flat against the outer ring, cold breath of air fogging from the seam, harsh noon light raking across the relief to show every chisel mark, 50mm lens
```
