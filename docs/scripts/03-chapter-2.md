# SHADOWS OF KANCHI
## Chapter 2: "The Drowned Stepwell"

**Runtime:** about 40 minutes · **Region:** the Kalyani, the great temple tank under the city · **Arc stage:** 2, the lone fixer
**New power:** Shadow Anchor (leave your shadow behind to hold a plate, lever or gate) · **Boss:** the Stepwell Serpent
**Introduces:** Kayal

---

**EXT. KANCHI, TEMPLE STREET. NOON. (THE STOPPED HOUR)**

ILAN walks down the middle of a temple street past stone vendors: a woman holding out a cone of puffed rice to nobody, a betel-seller with his knife raised over a leaf. Brass pots shine on the stone stalls.

NIZHAL walks on the shaded side of the street, stepping only in shade, like a child avoiding cracks.

> **NIZHAL**
> When the Mirror broke, the pieces flew. The nearest went down.

> **ILAN**
> Down where?

Nizhal points with his whole arm. At the end of the street, the ground just stops at a square of balustrade, and below it is a great green void.

---

**EXT. THE KALYANI, RIM. CONTINUOUS.**

Wide (24mm). The temple tank: a square pit three hundred feet across, its four walls made of stone steps in zigzag flights, nine levels deep, descending to green water. Each level has a pillared corridor and small shrines cut into the walls. At the centre of the water stands a small stone pavilion, the neerazhi mandapam, half drowned.

Shafts of noon sun drop straight in, so every step overhang throws a black wedge of shade. The tank is a cathedral built from shadow, and it's the most beautiful place Ilan has seen since the curse.

Far below, something glints violet under the water near the pavilion.

> **ILAN (V.O.)**
> The Kalyani was older than the city. Kanchi bathed in it every Masi Magam.

> **ILAN (V.O.)**
> I'd never been past the third step. Only priests went lower.

> **NIZHAL**
> Priests aren't here.

> **GAMEPLAY: THE DESCENT (practise Shadow Step)**
>
> Zigzag down the step-flights, Stepping between overhang shadows. The sun is straight above, so shade sits only directly under each overhang: thin bands that reward precise Steps. Six levels down in a few minutes. It should feel fast and confident. Ilan is getting good, and the camera lets him show off.

---

**EXT. THE KALYANI, LEVEL 6. CONTINUOUS.**

A grinding sound under the water. A stone sluice gate in the tank wall, frozen half-open for three hundred years, gives way. Water pours in from the old channel.

The tank begins to fill fast. Level 6 floods, then 5.

> **GAMEPLAY: RISING WATER (chase, no fail at first)**
>
> Climb back up as the water rises. Flooded steps lose their shade, because water takes the shadows. Each Step target vanishes as the water reaches it. At level 4, the rope Ilan is climbing snaps.

ILAN goes under. Silence. Green light. Bubbles.

Underwater, Ilan's shadow drifts beside him, black in the green. NIZHAL looks at him. He could reach out: Ilan sees it, and Nizhal sees him see it. He doesn't move.

> **NIZHAL**
> *(underwater, his voice clear and close)*
> I wondered what happens to me if you drown.

An **ARROW** punches through the water beside Ilan's hand, trailing a rope, and bites into a crack in the stone. Ilan grabs the rope. He is hauled up.

---

**EXT. THE KALYANI, LEVEL 3 CORRIDOR. CONTINUOUS.**

ILAN rolls onto dry stone, coughing water. Standing over him with a short bow, a second arrow nocked and aimed at his face, is **KAYAL (19)**.

Indigo cotton tied up for climbing. Jasmine braided into her hair, half of it crushed. Ink on two fingers. A palm-leaf satchel. She's soaked to the knee and furious.

> **KAYAL**
> You're the carver's son. The one who opened Mayan's door.

> **ILAN**
> How do you...

> **KAYAL**
> I'm the keeper's daughter. My father is stone at the temple doors.

> **KAYAL**
> I was down here when it happened. Underground. In the dark.

> **KAYAL**
> So I'm the only one left who knows what that Mirror is. And you're the fool who broke it.

She lowers the bow, but she doesn't put the arrow away.

> **ILAN**
> I can fix it.

> **KAYAL**
> *(a short laugh)*
> You can't climb out of a bath.

ILAN reaches into his sash and holds out the half-burnt palm-leaf letter. KAYAL takes it. She reads it once, and her face changes.

> **KAYAL**
> This is my father's hand.

> **ILAN**
> He sent it to the palace this morning. The minister took it from me.

> **KAYAL**
> *(very quiet)*
> And you gave it to him.

A long beat. Water drips. She puts the arrow back in her quiver.

> **KAYAL**
> The shard's at the bottom. I can't reach it. You can't swim.

> **KAYAL**
> We go down together. I don't trust you. Don't make me regret the rope.

> **ILAN (V.O.)**
> We agreed to work together. Neither of us meant it.

---

**INT. THE KALYANI, GATE ROOM. CONTINUOUS.**

A stone chamber in the tank wall: the old water-control room. A huge stone sluice gate held by a counterweight, and a pressure plate on the floor, carved with a fish.

> **KAYAL**
> The plate opens the gate. Someone has to stand on it.

> **ILAN**
> Then I'm on the wrong side.

NIZHAL walks onto the plate and stands on it. Nothing happens: he has no weight.

> **NIZHAL**
> I'm not heavy enough. I'm not anything.

> **KAYAL**
> *(not looking up from a carving on the wall)*
> "The shadow keeps the place the body leaves." It's written right here.

> **KAYAL**
> Your shadow can stay where you put it. If you let it.

> **GAMEPLAY: SHADOW ANCHOR (teach)**
>
> - Hold **STEP** while standing still to **Anchor**: Nizhal sinks into the floor as a flat pool of shadow and stays there, and the shadow has weight now. The plate goes down and the gate opens.
> - Ilan can now walk away from his shadow, up to 20 m. The further he goes, the paler his skin looks and the more muffled the sound gets (he's incomplete).
> - Release, or press STEP again, to call Nizhal back.
> - While Anchored, Ilan **cannot Step**. Every Anchor puzzle is a trade-off between the two.

> **NIZHAL**
> *(from the floor, flat and unhappy)*
> I'm a doorstop now.

---

**GAMEPLAY BLOCKS: THE TANK (about 20 min)**

> **The nine levels are one big puzzle about water.** Raising the water drowns the lower levels' shade but lifts floating things; lowering it exposes new steps and new shadows.
>
> 1. **Twin plates:** one plate in shade, one in sun. Anchor on the shaded one, stand on the sunny one yourself.
> 2. **Grille beams:** sunlight through stone lattice windows (jaali) makes strips of light that cut off shade. Anchor on a lever to close a shutter and kill one beam at a time.
> 3. **Kayal's ropes:** Kayal shoots rope arrows into carved rings (the player aims by pointing Ilan at a ring and tapping Cut). Ropes make bridges; a sagging rope throws a thin shadow on the level below.
> 4. **The floating lamp-boats:** the tank is littered with festival lamp-boats from Masi Magam: small rafts of banana stem and leaf with clay lamps, frozen afloat. Raise the water and they float to new places. Their tiny shadows become Step targets. Kayal: "My father floated one for my mother every year."
> 5. **The three-gate lock:** raise the water three levels by holding a gate with the Anchor while you cross the flooding room before it closes.
>
> **Shrine niche (story beat, optional):** a small shrine to the river goddess, with an inscription Kayal reads aloud:
> *"Water remembers what stone forgets."*
> If Ilan stands with her for 10 seconds, she adds: "My father made me learn every inscription in this tank. I hated it."

---

**EXT. THE KALYANI, DROWNED COURT. LATER.**

The water is low now. The bottom of the tank: a square court of cracked flagstones around the half-drowned pavilion. Shapes lie everywhere: the shadows of everyone who ever drowned or bathed here, pooled together on the floor like spilled ink.

They begin to move together.

> **KAYAL**
> Ilan. The floor.

The ink rises, and keeps rising. It braids itself into one body, thick as a palm trunk, looping around the pavilion pillars: **THE STEPWELL SERPENT**. A naga of shadow and water, its head crowned with the dead shapes of old floating lamps.

> **NIZHAL**
> Oh, I don't like him at all.

> **BOSS: THE STEPWELL SERPENT (3 phases)**
>
> - **Phase 1: the flood.** The serpent lives in water. It lunges from the pool and drags itself back. Anchor on one drain gate and stand on the other to drain the pool. Exposed to the noon sun, it recoils and smokes.
> - **Phase 2: the coil.** It wraps the pavilion pillars, where the shade protects it. Step into a pillar's shadow behind it and Anchor; Kayal shoots the rope that drops the pavilion's stone grille, cutting its coil in two with a band of sunlight.
> - **Phase 3: the sun.** Fully drained, it thrashes in full light. Kayal pins its head with three arrows. **Ilan has no powers here** (no shade anywhere). He runs across the burning court as an ordinary man, climbs its neck and pulls the violet shard from its throat.

The serpent collapses into a thousand separate shadows, which slide away into the cracks between the stones.

---

**EXT. THE KALYANI, DROWNED COURT. CONTINUOUS.**

ILAN holds the shard. It's warm. He presses it to his armband, and it slides in beside the first like a key into a lock. His armband glows. Nizhal's outline sharpens.

Water sheets off the pavilion. On its inner wall, newly uncovered, is an old Grantha carving. KAYAL reads it silently. Close on her eyes as she reads it twice. Insert, the carving:

*"The one who mends the Mirror gives it his shadow. Kanchi will not remember his name."*

> **ILAN**
> What does it say?

> **KAYAL**
> *(a beat too long)*
> Old prayers. Nothing useful.

She turns away. NIZHAL, behind her, has read it too. He looks at Ilan, says nothing, and smiles.

> **ILAN (V.O.)**
> I asked her later what the carving said. She lied again.

> **ILAN (V.O.)**
> I didn't blame her. I'd have lied to me too.

The shard pulses. The water at their feet goes bright, like sun on a river, and the sound of drums comes up through it, very far off.

> **KAYAL**
> What is that?

> **NIZHAL**
> Someone's memory. The shard's full of them.

## Death and restart lines (stage 2, lone fixer)

1. "I got up. I always get up."
2. "I didn't need the rope. Fine. I needed the rope."
3. "Kayal would say I'm telling it too fast."

## Lore inscriptions

1. *"Water remembers what stone forgets."* (river-goddess shrine)
2. *"The shadow keeps the place the body leaves."* (gate room; teaches Anchor)
3. *"On Masi Magam the whole city bathes here, king and potter in the same water."* (level 3)
4. *"Nine steps down for nine planets. Count them, and you will not drown."* (level 9)
5. *"The one who mends the Mirror gives it his shadow. Kanchi will not remember his name."* (pavilion; Kayal hides it)

## Accuracy notes

- The tank is modelled on Tamil temple tanks (Mahamaham tank in Kumbakonam, the Kanchipuram temple tanks) with a central neerazhi mandapam, not on the stepwells of Gujarat or Rajasthan.
- **Masi Magam** is a real festival of sacred bathing in temple tanks; the floating lamp-boats are period-appropriate (banana stem rafts, clay lamps, flowers).
- Jaali (pierced stone screens) and pillared tank corridors are authentic to South Indian temple architecture.
