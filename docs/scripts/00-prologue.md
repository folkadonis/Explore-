# Prologue: The Last Morning

**Region:** Royal Palace rooftops · **Length:** about 10 min · **Arc stage:** 1, the proud boy
**Teaches:** run, jump, wall-run, ledge grab · **Powers unlocked:** none

Rule for all lines: under 15 words, no modern slang, mythic but warm. Ilan narrates in past tense.

---

## Scene 0: The frame

> Two versions. **A** follows the story bible. **B** is the original frame proposed in `docs/story-notes.md` (fix B). Pick one; the rest of the script works with both.

**A: River at dusk**
*Wide shot. The Kaveri at dusk, gold water, a ferry pole knocking against a jetty. An older ILAN (about 30) sits on the steps of a bathing ghat. Camera slowly pushes in past his shoulder.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** Most people think Prince Aditya's coronation was an ordinary day.
> **ILAN (V.O.):** I'm going to tell you what really happened.

**B: The carved wall**
*Close on a chisel biting granite. Pull back: older ILAN on a bamboo scaffold against the Mirror Temple's outer wall. The frieze beside him shows a palace roof and a running boy. Dusk light.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** My father built this wall. Nobody remembers his name either.
> **ILAN (V.O.):** So I'll carve what happened. Someone should know.

*Match cut: the carved running boy becomes young Ilan, mid-leap across a rooftop.*

## Scene 1: Rooftop run (tutorial)

*Morning. Low sun, marigold garlands strung between towers. Third-person chase camera behind and slightly above ILAN, 20, sprinting along a ridge of roof tiles. Below, crowds pour into the coronation courtyard.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** I was the fastest runner in Kanchi. Ask anyone.
> **ILAN (V.O.):** That morning I carried a message for the temple. And I was late.

**Tutorial beats** (each gets one loading-screen line later)
1. Run along the roof ridge. Conch shells sound below.
2. Jump a gap between towers. *ILAN (V.O.): "Guards don't jump. I did."*
3. Wall-run along the painted palace gallery. Pigeons scatter.
4. Ledge grab onto a gopuram tier. *ILAN (V.O.): "My father carved these. I knew every handhold."*
5. Slide down a banner rope into the courtyard's back rows.

*Camera rises: the full courtyard. Drums. Prince ADITYA, 20, on the dais, the crown on a bronze tray before him.*

---

## Scene 2: Overlooked

*ILAN squeezes to the front of the guards' row. A senior GUARD blocks him with a spear shaft.*

> **GUARD:** Back row, carver's boy.
> **ILAN:** I grew up with him.
> **GUARD:** So did half the city.

*ADITYA's eyes pass over the crowd. They pass over Ilan, too. Ilan lifts a hand, then lowers it.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** He didn't see me. I told myself he couldn't.

---

## Scene 3: The minister's request

*A cloister behind the courtyard, cool and shaded. KAALAVAN, 55, in silk, waits beside a pillar. His voice is low and kind.*

> **KAALAVAN:** You're Sengan's son. The stone-carver.
> **ILAN:** You knew my father?
> **KAALAVAN:** He built the Mirror Temple's wall. Mayan's door knows his blood.
> **KAALAVAN:** The new king must look into the Mirror today. The keepers are too slow.
> **KAALAVAN:** Bring it down before noon. Only you can open the door.
> **ILAN:** Why me?
> **KAALAVAN:** Do this, and your name goes in stone. Beside the kings.

*ILAN looks up at the Mirror Temple, black and thirteen storeys high, over the city.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** I should have asked why the keepers weren't told.
> **ILAN (V.O.):** I didn't ask anything. I ran.

*Cut to black. Title card: SHADOWS OF KANCHI.*

## Death and restart lines (stage 1, cocky)

1. "Wait. I'm telling it wrong."
2. "I'd never fall for that. Again."
3. "Ask anyone. I didn't fall."

## Environmental puzzle ideas (no powers yet)

1. **Garland swing:** a marigold garland strung between two towers sags under Ilan's weight. Run along it before it tears; stop and it drops you.
2. **Pigeon tell:** pigeons sit on the safe, solid tiles. Loose tiles have none. The player learns to read the roof by watching birds.

## Lore inscriptions (Kayal reads these later; here they're carved roof tablets)

1. *"Mayan raised this tower in one lifetime. He did not live to see it shine."*
2. *"The king looks once. The Mirror looks back forever."*
3. *"Sengan, carver, set these stones. — added later, in a smaller hand"* (Ilan's father, carved by a fellow mason)
4. *"Whatever is lost lives in its shadow. Whatever is shadow, the Mirror keeps."*
5. *"Do not wake the Mirror for want. It answers want with cost."*
