# Chapter 1: The Coronation of Ash

**Region:** Royal Palace and Mirror Temple outer face · **Length:** about 35 min · **Arc stage:** 1, the proud boy
**New power:** Shadow Step · **Boss / set piece:** escape the collapsing coronation hall

The playable prototype in `prototype/` covers scenes 3 to 6.

---

## Scene 1: Mayan's door

*Noon approaching. ILAN climbs the Mirror Temple's outer face. Camera swings wide to show the city shrinking below, then tight on his hands. At the top: a circular door of carved stone rings, each ring a band of figures (elephants, dancers, stars, serpents).*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** Mayan's door. My father said it only opened for family.
> **ILAN:** Then open, stone. I'm family.

**Puzzle:** rotate the three rings so the carved figures form one procession, an elephant leading a dancer leading a star. Each ring turns with a grinding click, and dust falls on a correct alignment.

*The door rolls aside. Darkness, then violet light. The NIZHAL KANNADI (Shadow Mirror) floats in a cage of bronze, a black obsidian disc taller than a man. Shapes move inside it.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** Inside it, I saw every shadow Kanchi ever cast.
> **ILAN (V.O.):** Mothers. Soldiers. Children who grew up and died. All moving.

---

## Scene 2: The shattering

*ILAN lifts the Mirror from its cradle. It's lighter than it looks. Footsteps. KAALAVAN steps out of the door's shadow, a bronze rod in hand.*

> **ILAN:** Minister? I have it. I'll carry it down myself.
> **KAALAVAN:** You've done enough, boy. More than you know.

*He strikes. The Mirror cracks with a sound like ice on a river. Slow motion: shards spin outward. One buries itself in ILAN's bronze armband. A ring of darkness rolls out over Kanchi.*

*Exterior, wide: the wave sweeps the city. Every shadow peels off the ground like cloth in wind. People freeze grey mid-step. The sun jerks to the top of the sky and stops.*

*Black.*

---

## Scene 3: Waking (prototype starts here)

*Temple steps. ILAN wakes face-down. The sun is fixed overhead. Silence, no birds. His shadow lies beside him, then sits up on its own.*

> **NIZHAL:** Well. You've really done it this time.
> **ILAN:** Who said that?
> **NIZHAL:** Look down. Or don't. I'm not there anymore.
> **ILAN:** You're my shadow.
> **NIZHAL:** I was. Now I'm your shadow with opinions.
> **NIZHAL:** Keep up. The palace is coming down.

---

## Scene 4: Learning to step

*The palace terraces. Long gaps where roofs have fallen. The only shade comes from banners, frozen elephants and stone citizens.*

> **NIZHAL:** Too far to jump. Not too far for me.
> **ILAN:** What does that mean?
> **NIZHAL:** Find a shadow. Want to be in it. Trust me for once.

*Tutorial: the first Shadow Step into a banner's shadow. On success:*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** It felt like falling upward. Like being folded into ink.
> **NIZHAL:** You're welcome.

**Statue moment (optional):** stop beside a frozen flower girl.
> **ILAN:** ...We should keep moving.
*(Stage 1 Ilan doesn't look twice. In Ch. 5, he'll know her name: Malar.)*

---

## Scene 5: The sunlit courtyard (key puzzle)

*A broad courtyard in full noon sun. Silk canopies, rolled tight for the procession, hang from rope posts.*

> **NIZHAL:** No shade out there. Then make some.
> **ILAN:** Those canopies cost more than my father earned in a year.
> **NIZHAL:** He's not using the money.

*Cut the ropes. Canopies unroll and their shadows fall across the far ledges. Step from shade to shade.*

> **ILAN (after first cut):** Silk for the prince's procession. Shade for me.

---

## Scene 6: The coronation hall (set piece)

*The pillared hall where the feast was laid out. Under the roof it's dark. Too dark. Shapes peel off the walls: NIZHALARS, the torn shadows, walking.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** The hall was dark. And it was not empty.
> **NIZHAL:** They want bodies back. Yours will do.
> **NIZHAL:** Run. They can't follow you into the sun.

*Chase: pillars crack, the roof sheds stones. Ilan Steps between pillars while walkers close in behind. He bursts into the light; the walkers reach the threshold and burn into ash.*

---

## Scene 7: End beat

*The main courtyard. ADITYA, stone, crown half-raised toward his own head. Ilan walks up slowly. Camera holds behind Ilan's shoulder, Aditya's frozen face in focus.*

> **ILAN (V.O.):** We climbed the same walls as boys. He never once looked down on me.
> **NIZHAL:** Nobody saw it was you. You could just walk away.
> **ILAN:** I could have.
> **ILAN (V.O.):** I didn't.

*He touches the stone crown and turns toward the city. Far below, the great stepwell glints. Chapter card: II · THE DROWNED STEPWELL.*

---

## Death and restart lines (stage 1, cocky)

1. "Wait. I'm telling it wrong."
2. "I'd never fall for that."
3. "I was the best climber in Kanchi. Ask anyone."

## Environmental puzzle ideas (Shadow Step)

1. **Elephant procession:** frozen war elephants stand on broken terrace islands. Their bellies throw the only shade. Step elephant to elephant over the gaps. The last one stands in a spot where its shadow only reaches the far ledge if you first knock its stone parasol down.
2. **Swinging lamp gallery:** a chain of bronze lamps, still swinging from the shockwave, casts moving shadows across a gallery floor. Time Steps to the swing. In the prototype this is a swinging banner.

## Lore inscriptions

1. *"Here the procession of King Rajendra halted. The elephants knelt to the sun."* (palace terrace)
2. *"Mayan's door asks one question: whose hands built me?"* (temple outer face)
3. *"Shade is a gift. Give it to the traveller and the stranger."* (canopy posts)
4. *"The coronation lamp is lit once. It is not put out."* (hall, ironic: the lamps are dark)
5. *"Under the hall sleep the old wells. Water remembers."* (exit arch, points to Ch. 2)
