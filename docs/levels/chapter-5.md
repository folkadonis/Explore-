# Chapter 5 level: The Mirror Temple

**Powers:** all four → **Eclipse** (3 charges, refilled at shrines) · **Target length:** 50 min · **Checkpoints:** ⛳
**Teach / practise / test:** rooms 2 to 3 teach, rooms 4 to 9 practise, rooms 10 to 12 test.

The 13-storey tower floats in broken pieces in a violet sky. Each floor shows a shadow-memory from Kanchi's past: the people Ilan is saving. Floors rotate slowly, so sun direction changes as you climb. This is the only chapter where shadows don't always lean the same way.

**Final boss: Nizhal (3 phases)**
1. **Shadow vs. shadow.** Nizhal has all of Ilan's powers. He Steps, Anchors and Strikes. Mirror his moves; Strike him when he Anchors (he's split in two and weaker).
2. **In full light.** Kayal lights the summit torches. No shadows, no powers. Ilan fights as a normal man with his short blade; Nizhal is weaker in light too. Stamina-based duel. The narration is quiet here.
3. **Letting go.** Nizhal, beaten, begs. The power bar is full. The prompt is not "Strike" but "Release." Holding the button drains the power bar to zero for good while Nizhal fades into the Mirror. If the player doesn't press it, Nizhal slowly recovers, and a line repeats: "You could keep me. Nobody would know." The game waits for you.

**Shadow-memory collectible:** floor 8. *Sengan, stone-carver, Ilan's father. Carved his son's initial in the wall's lowest stone.* (Ilan finds it.)
