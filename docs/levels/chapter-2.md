# Chapter 2 level: Kalyani Stepwell

**Powers:** Shadow Step → **Shadow Anchor** · **Target length:** 40 min · **Checkpoints:** ⛳
**Teach / practise / test:** rooms 4 to 5 teach, rooms 6 to 8 practise, rooms 9 to 11 test.

The stepwell is nine levels of stone steps around a square shaft of green water. Water level is the core variable: high water drowns lower ledges and their shadows; low water exposes them.

```
 L1  [1 Rim] → [2 Descent]
 L3  [3 Rising water] ⟶ Kayal saves Ilan
 L4  [4 Gate room] → [5 Twin plates]
 L5  [6 Light shafts] → [7 Rope bridges]
 L7  [8 Shrine niches] → [9 Three-gate lock]
 L9  [10 Drowned court] → [11 Serpent pool]
```

**Boss: Stepwell Serpent (3 phases)**
1. It lunges from the water. Anchor two drain gates open (one with your shadow, one with your body) to lower the pool. Sunlight hits it and it recoils.
2. It coils around a pillar. Step behind it into the pillar's shadow, Anchor, and Kayal shoots the rope that drops a grate.
3. Full drain. It thrashes in the sun; Kayal pins it. Ilan walks into the light (no powers) and pulls the shard from its throat.

**Shadow-memory collectible:** room 8. *Vel, water-carrier, 60, carried water up 300 steps a day for forty years.*
