# Chapter 3 level: Bronze Foundry

**Powers:** Step, Anchor → **Shadow Strike** · **Target length:** 45 min · **Checkpoints:** ⛳
**Teach / practise / test:** rooms 3 to 4 teach, rooms 5 to 8 practise, rooms 9 to 11 test.

Furnace glow pulses on a slow rhythm (about 4 seconds). When the furnaces flare, orange light wipes out nearby shadows. Players learn to count the breath of the foundry.

**Boss: Bronze Colossus (30-foot war statue, climbed like a level)**
1. It stomps the yard. Step into the shadow of its raised foot, Strike the ankle seal. Repeat on both legs to topple it to one knee.
2. Climb its arm. Anchor to hold a loose armour plate open; Strike the elbow core.
3. On its shoulders, the core glows in the chest. When the furnace behind flares, its own body shades the chest: Step in and Strike the core.

**Shadow-memory collectible:** room 6, at the top of the gear tower. *Ponni, bell-caster, cast the bell that rings the coronation. Never heard it.*
