# Chapter 4 level: Thousand-Pillar Hall

**Powers:** Step, Anchor, Strike → **Light Bend** · **Target length:** 45 min · **Checkpoints:** ⛳
**Teach / practise / test:** rooms 2 to 3 teach, rooms 4 to 6 practise, rooms 7 to 9 test.

Library and observatory. Moonlight pours through a domed skylight (the shard here makes local night), so this is the only chapter with a second light source the player controls. Lamp-bearers carry violet-white shard-light that cancels shadows: kill them first.

Nizhal is turning in this chapter. One in six Steps lands slightly off target (story-notes fix 4). Never off a ledge: always a safe nearby shadow.

**Duel: Kaalavan's projection (survival, about 90 seconds)**
- Goal on screen: "Protect the shard pedestal until the pillars align."
- He fires shard-light that wipes shadows in a line. Bend mirrors to keep at least one pillar shadow near the pedestal.
- Strike him from shadow to stagger him and buy time. He can't be killed.
- When the timer ends, the pillars align and the shard is exposed. Cutscene: he takes it anyway.

**Shadow-memory collectible:** room 5, on the highest walkway. *Arivu, star-reader, mapped a comet no one else saw. Named it after her mother.*
