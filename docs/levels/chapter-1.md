# Chapter 1 level: Royal Palace

**Powers:** none → Shadow Step · **Target length:** 35 min · **Checkpoints:** ⛳
**Teach / practise / test:** rooms 4 to 5 teach, rooms 6 to 8 practise, rooms 9 to 10 test.

```
 [1 Door]→[2 Shattering]→[3 Waking]→[4 First gap]→[5 Statue garden]
                                                     ↓
 [10 Aditya]←[9 Hall escape]←[8 Canopy court]←[7 Banner gallery]←[6 Elephant terraces]
```

| # | Room | Traversal challenge | Light and shadow | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mayan's Door | Climb the temple face, then the ring-rotation puzzle | Pre-curse: normal morning light | ⛳ Story only, no fail state on the puzzle |
| 2 | Mirror chamber | Walk to the Mirror; cutscene | Violet glow from the Mirror | Cutscene: shattering |
| 3 | Temple steps | Short climb down broken steps | Sun locks at noon; shadows vanish from people | ⛳ Nizhal wakes |
| 4 | First gap | One gap too wide to jump | A single banner throws shade on the far side | **Teach Shadow Step.** Can't fail: falling respawns you at the edge |
| 5 | Statue garden | Low walls, short gaps | Every statue casts a small shadow; chain 3 short Steps | Shows that stone casts shade. ⛳ |
| 6 | Elephant terraces | Four terrace islands over a drop | Frozen elephants' bellies are the only shade | **Practise.** Last elephant: knock its parasol down to stretch its shadow |
| 7 | Banner gallery | Long gap under swinging banners | Moving shadows sweep the far ledge | **Practise timing.** ⛳ before |
| 8 | Canopy court | Wide sunlit courtyard | No shade until you cut rope posts to drop canopies | **Key puzzle.** Two canopies, the second only reachable after the first. ⛳ |
| 9 | Coronation hall | Run the full hall length | Whole interior is shade, so Step anywhere, but Nizhal Walkers live in it | **Test / set piece.** Walkers chase; burn at the sunlit exit. Falling roof stones. ⛳ before |
| 10 | Main courtyard | Walk to Aditya's statue | Full sun, quiet | End beat. No enemies |

**Shadow-memory collectible:** room 5, behind the flower-seller's statue, reachable only by Stepping into the shade of her basket. Memory: *Malar, 9, sells garlands, saving for a bronze bell.*

**Checkpoint spacing:** never more than 2 minutes of play between checkpoints; always one before a timing challenge.
